Marktone

A desktop Markdown editor for macOS and Linux that shows your document rendered as you write it, and saves it as plain Markdown.

Download

Version 0.56.7

New since 0.56.3

  • Find controls at the edge of the document. Move the pointer into the top or bottom row to reveal its controls independently. Zoom, theme and Read or Edit are grouped above the document. Update messages stay centred at the bottom even when the controls fade.
  • Open and resize side panels. The folder sidebar, Outline and Settings have separate toggles. Outline and Settings share the right side. Panels keep their allocated width as they slide and can be resized from 300 to 480 px. The window minimum is 980 px and both panels fit from 1,280 px.
  • Fill and resize Mac document windows from the title bar. Documents open in the display's usable area. Double-click the blank title area to switch to a centred window at 70% of the usable width and height, within the minimum window size. Double-click again to fill the available area.
  • Read with consistent spacing. The default text column is 800 px at Actual Size. The paragraph line height defaults to 160% of the body font size in Day and Night.
  • See where typing will begin. A new blank document opens with a blinking insertion point in the theme's accent colour. Click the editable text or a blank line to place the caret.
  • Find and adjust Settings more easily. The Interface font setting names what it changes. Controls have at least 160 px of width, with 40 px of space before them and at the right edge. Controls move below their descriptions when needed and have corner radii of at most 2 px. Slider values appear only while dragging. The bottom row now holds the version and update controls.
  • Update and pick up where you left off. On installations Marktone can update, one click on Update saves open documents, verifies and installs the download, then restarts and reopens saved documents. If a document cannot be saved, Marktone pauses the install or restart.
  • Find About information in Help. The read-only Help window now starts with the app logo, version and website links, followed by the command and shortcut reference.

macOS (Apple silicon)

↓ Marktone-arm64.dmg 19.4 MB · SHA-256

Open the .dmg and drag Marktone into Applications. The first time, right-click Marktone in Applications and choose Open: Marktone is not notarised by Apple, so macOS refuses a plain double-click.

Linux (x86-64)

Install Marktone, or update it, with one line in a terminal. It puts Marktone, its menu entry and its icon in your own folders under ~/.local, and needs no sudo:

curl -fsSL https://marktone.app/install.sh | sh

Remove it with this line. It removes only what the installer put in place, and keeps your settings and your Markdown files:

curl -fsSL https://marktone.app/uninstall.sh | sh

Or download a package:

↓ Marktone-x86_64.AppImage 26.2 MB · SHA-256
Make it executable with chmod +x Marktone-x86_64.AppImage, then run it.

↓ Marktone-amd64.deb 27.7 MB · SHA-256
On Debian or Ubuntu: sudo apt install ./Marktone-amd64.deb

↓ Marktone-x86_64.tar.gz 27.7 MB · SHA-256
Unpack it and run marktone in the unpacked folder; its desktop file and icon are beside it.
