Marktone

A desktop Markdown editor for macOS and Linux that shows your document rendered as you write it, and saves it as plain Markdown.

Version 0.83.8

New in writing

  • Skeletons to start from. A new Insert Skeleton icon at the top of the window, between Zoom In and Read or Edit, puts a ready-made heading, table, list or task list into the document, ready to fill in. A list skeleton placed beside a list of its kind takes another marker, so it stays a list of its own.
  • Empty lines stay where you made them. Each Enter on an empty line, or after the last paragraph, now moves the caret down one more line, and the empty lines keep their space when you start typing: the text no longer jumps back up under the paragraph before it. Up and Down stop on the empty lines too.
  • Spaces and the caret on empty lines. Spaces typed on an empty line stay with the text you type after them, the caret is drawn on that line after a space, and Backspace after typing on an empty line leaves the document exactly as it was before.

New in the look

  • Everything on the 8 px step. Every space between blocks, and every row, field and gap in the window, is now a multiple of 8 px at Actual Size: 16 px around paragraphs, lists and tables, 8 px between list items, 24 px around quotes, 32 px rows in menus and the side panels. Line heights stay a ratio of the font size, as before.
  • A smaller default size. The document's text is 13 px by default, where it was 14.
  • The text runs down to the foot of the window. The document's last line now stands just above the bottom row of icons, not 80 px above it.
  • Nested files and headings sit closer. Each level of the file list and the outline steps 24 px in, where it stepped 40.
  • The find bar is compact. Find and replace sit in one bar 600 px wide, centred over the text, instead of spreading across the whole window.
  • Chinese and Japanese in their own fonts. Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and Korean are each drawn in the font your system uses for that language, as the system's own apps draw them, where before all of them were drawn in a Simplified Chinese font.

New in the window and its icons

  • The icons are always shown. The rows of icons at the top and bottom of the window are visible from the start, without hovering over them. Turn on Auto-hide toolbar in Settings to have them fade until you move to them.
  • Theme and Help move to the bottom row, making room for Insert Skeleton at the top.

New in Help

  • Help in four languages. Help is written in British English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese, and opens in the first of your system's languages it is written in, else in British English. Its examples are translated too; commands, shortcuts and skeleton names stay in English.
  • Templates in every section. Each how-to section of Help carries a skeleton you can copy, and shortcuts are shown as code.

New in the app

  • A new icon: the Dark Back logo, two gold bars across a black square, crossed by two slanted gold strokes. Help's About page shows it too.
  • British English throughout. The interface is written in British English, such as Window → Minimise.

Open the .dmg and drag Marktone into Applications. The first time, right-click Marktone in Applications and choose Open: Marktone is not notarised by Apple, so macOS refuses a plain double-click.

Linux (x86-64)

Install Marktone, or update it, with one line in a terminal. It puts Marktone, its menu entry and its icon in your own folders under ~/.local, and needs no sudo:

curl -fsSL https://marktone.app/install.sh | sh

Remove it with this line. It removes only what the installer put in place, and keeps your settings and your Markdown files:

curl -fsSL https://marktone.app/uninstall.sh | sh

Or download a package:

↓ Marktone-x86_64.AppImage 23.9 MB · SHA-256
For a portable copy, make it executable with chmod +x Marktone-x86_64.AppImage, then run it. To install the AppImage with a menu entry and Markdown file associations, use curl -fsSL https://marktone.app/install.sh | sh -s -- --appimage.

The Problem

Plain Markdown is easy to keep and hard to read: every heading, emphasis and table is wrapped in #, ** and |. A separate preview pane means reading in one place and writing in another.

What Marktone Does

Shows the document, not the markup. Headings, emphasis, lists, quotes, tables, code, links, pictures, task lists and footnotes are drawn as they read. The block under the caret shows its Markdown, so you always edit the real text.

Saves as you go, and changes only what you edited. Every change is saved to the file without asking. Files with Windows line endings are saved with Unix line endings. If another program changes the file you are editing, Marktone tells you.

Reads comfortably, day and night. A day theme and a night theme follow your system's light or dark setting. Settings sets the font (Berkeley Mono Variable when installed), its size and weight, the line height and the width of the text column.

Works across a folder. Open Folder lists a folder's Markdown files in a sidebar, and the outline lists the document's headings, taking you to one with a click. Find and Replace work across the document, Chinese included.

Helps you write. Brackets, quotes and ** close as you type, copying gives Markdown, and a task's checkbox ticks with a click. A pasted picture is saved in a <document name>.assets folder beside the document and linked.

Exports to HTML and PDF. Export as HTML writes an HTML file. Export as PDF prints A4 pages through an installed Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave.

Marktone has no plugins, maths, diagrams, custom CSS or account.

When to Use Marktone

Keeping notes, drafts or a book in Markdown files, and wanting to read them rendered while you write.

Editing READMEs and documentation that must stay plain Markdown in the repository.

Writing in Chinese and English, with the system's input methods.
